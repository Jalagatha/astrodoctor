Soundscapes for the five VR theme worlds (60 s seamless loops, mono MP3, 96 kbps, ~-23 LUFS).

  calm.mp3      Garden at dusk  - warm A-major chord that swells with the 10 s breathing cycle,
                                  distant crickets, soft breeze, occasional chimes
  nature.mp3    Forest rain     - steady rain on leaves, close drips, low wind, breathing hum
  learning.mp3  Language lab    - calm focus pad with an unhurried music-box melody
  active.mp3    Football & bike - sea swell on the shore, lively birdsong, bright breeze
  family.mp3    School run      - morning bird chorus, rustling leaves, warm homely chord

All sounds are synthesised by tools/make_ambience.py from noise and oscillators: they are
original to this project and contain no third-party recordings. To change or rebuild them:

  python tools/make_ambience.py          (needs numpy, scipy and ffmpeg on PATH)
